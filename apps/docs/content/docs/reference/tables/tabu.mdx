---
title: tabu
description: 'Tables to a width with X[l], X[c] and X[r] columns.'
---

## Loading

```latex
\usepackage{tabu}
```

A module of its own, compiled into mLaTeX: the package's commands are defined over mLaTeX's primitives.

## Commands

`\endlongtabu`, `\endtabu`, `\everyrow`, `\longtabu`, `\rowfont`, `\tabu`, `\tabucline`, `\tabulinesep`, `\tabulinestyle`, `\tabuphantomline`, `\taburowcolors`, `\taburulecolor`.

## Example

A document that uses it, from mLaTeX's own tests, so it compiles as written, and below it what mLaTeX sets from it in your browser:

```latex title="tabu.tex" preview
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{tabu}
\begin{document}
\begin{tabu} to \linewidth {X[l] X[c] X[r]}
\rowfont{\bfseries} Left & Centre & Right \\
a & b & c \\
\end{tabu}
\begin{longtabu}{X X}a & b\\\end{longtabu}
\end{document}
```
