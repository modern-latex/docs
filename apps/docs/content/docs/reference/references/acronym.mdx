---
title: acronym
description: 'An abbreviation spelled out the first time it is used, and written short every time after.'
---

## Loading

```latex
\usepackage{acronym}
```

A module of its own, compiled into mLaTeX: the package's commands are defined over mLaTeX's primitives.

## Commands

`\ac`, `\acf`, `\acl`, `\acp`, `\acreset`, `\acro`, `\acrodef`, `\acs`, `\newacronym`.

## Example

A document that uses it, from mLaTeX's own tests, so it compiles as written, and below it what mLaTeX sets from it in your browser:

```latex title="acronym.tex" preview
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{acronym}
\begin{document}
First \ac{nasa}, then \ac{nasa} again, and \acl{esa} in full, \acs{esa} short, \acf{who} in full with its short form.
\begin{acronym}
\acro{nasa}[NASA]{National Aeronautics and Space Administration}
\acro{esa}[ESA]{European Space Agency}
\acro{who}[WHO]{World Health Organization}
\end{acronym}
\end{document}
```
