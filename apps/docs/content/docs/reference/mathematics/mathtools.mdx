## Loading

```latex
\usepackage{mathtools}
```

A module of its own, compiled into mLaTeX: the package's commands are defined over mLaTeX's primitives.

## Commands

`\Aboxed`, `\adjustlimits`, `\allowdisplaybreaks`, `\boxed`, `\ceil`, `\cfrac`, `\Coloneqq`, `\coloneqq`, `\cramped`, `\dblcolon`, `\DeclarePairedDelimiter`, `\DeclarePairedDelimiterX`, `\displaybreak`, `\eqqcolon`, `\genfrac`, `\lceil`, `\lefteqn`, `\lVert`, `\lvert`, `\mathclap`, `\mathllap`, `\mathmakebox`, `\mathrlap`, `\mathtoolsset`, `\MoveEqLeft`, `\mspace`, `\newtagform`, `\nobreakdash`, `\prescript`, `\rceil`, `\rVert`, `\rvert`, `\shortintertext`, `\shoveleft`, `\shoveright`, `\sideset`, `\smashoperator`, `\splitdfrac`, `\splitfrac`, `\underbracket`, `\usetagform`, `\vcentcolon`, `\xhookleftarrow`, `\xhookrightarrow`, `\xLeftarrow`, `\xleftarrow`, `\xLeftrightarrow`, `\xleftrightarrow`, `\xmapsto`, `\xRightarrow`, `\xrightarrow`, `\xrightharpoonup`, `\xrightleftharpoons`.

## Example

A document that uses it, from mLaTeX's own tests, so it compiles as written, and below it what mLaTeX sets from it in your browser:

```latex title="mathtools.tex" preview
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{mathtools}
\begin{document}
\DeclarePairedDelimiter\abs{\lvert}{\rvert}
\DeclarePairedDelimiter\norm{\lVert}{\rVert}
\DeclarePairedDelimiter\ceil{\lceil}{\rceil}
\[ a \coloneqq b \quad \abs{x} \quad \norm{v} \quad \xleftrightarrow{a} \quad \mathclap{x} \quad \prescript{a}{b}{X} \quad \underbracket{abc} \quad \smashoperator{\sum_{i=1}^n} \]
$\ceil{x}$ and $\ceil*{\frac{a}{b}}$.
\[ \begin{dcases} a & b \\ c & d \end{dcases} \]
\begin{align}
x &= y \\
\intertext{and} z &= w
\end{align}
\mathtoolsset{showonlyrefs}
\end{document}
```
