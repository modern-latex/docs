## Loading

```latex
\usepackage{amsmath}
```

A module of its own, compiled into mLaTeX: the package's commands are defined over mLaTeX's primitives.

## Commands

`\Aboxed`, `\adjustlimits`, `\allowdisplaybreaks`, `\binom`, `\bmod`, `\boxed`, `\cfrac`, `\Coloneqq`, `\coloneqq`, `\cramped`, `\dblcolon`, `\DeclareMathOperator`, `\DeclarePairedDelimiterX`, `\dfrac`, `\displaybreak`, `\eqqcolon`, `\eqref`, `\genfrac`, `\idotsint`, `\iint`, `\impliedby`, `\implies`, `\intertext`, `\lefteqn`, `\mathbin`, `\mathclap`, `\mathclose`, `\mathinner`, `\mathllap`, `\mathmakebox`, `\mathop`, `\mathopen`, `\mathord`, `\mathpunct`, `\mathrel`, `\mathrlap`, `\mathtoolsset`, `\min`, `\MoveEqLeft`, `\mspace`, `\newtagform`, `\nobreakdash`, `\notag`, `\numberwithin`, `\operatorname`, `\operatorwithlimits`, `\operatorwithout`, `\overset`, `\pmod`, `\pod`, `\prescript`, `\shortintertext`, `\shoveleft`, `\shoveright`, `\sideset`, `\smashoperator`, `\splitdfrac`, `\splitfrac`, `\tag`, `\text`, `\tfrac`, `\underset`, `\usetagform`, `\vcentcolon`, `\xhookleftarrow`, `\xhookrightarrow`, `\xLeftarrow`, `\xleftarrow`, `\xLeftrightarrow`, `\xleftrightarrow`, `\xmapsto`, `\xRightarrow`, `\xrightarrow`, `\xrightharpoonup`, `\xrightleftharpoons`.

## Example

A document that uses it, from mLaTeX's own tests, so it compiles as written, and below it what mLaTeX sets from it in your browser:

```latex title="amsmath.tex" preview
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{amsmath}
\begin{document}
\begin{align}
a &= b + c \label{eq:a} \\
d &= e \tag{*} \\
f &= g \notag
\end{align}
\begin{gather}
x = y \\ z = w
\end{gather}
\begin{multline}
p = q + r + s \\ + t + u
\end{multline}
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
a &= b \\ &= c
\end{split}
\end{equation}
\begin{alignat}{2}
x &= 1 &\quad y &= 2
\end{alignat}
\[ \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \quad \binom{n}{k} \quad \dfrac{1}{2} \quad \tfrac{1}{2} \quad \text{text} \quad \operatorname{sgn} x \quad \overset{!}{=} \quad \underset{x}{\min} \quad \xrightarrow{f} \quad \iint \quad \idotsint \]
\[ f(x) = \begin{cases} 1 & x > 0 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \]
Equation~\eqref{eq:a}, and \numberwithin{equation}{section} is read.
\end{document}
```
