## Loading

```latex
\usepackage{quantikz}
```

A module of its own, compiled into mLaTeX: the package's commands are defined over mLaTeX's primitives.

## Commands

`\ctrl`, `\gate`, `\ket`, `\lstick`, `\meter`, `\qw`, `\targ`.

## Example

A document that uses it, from mLaTeX's own tests, so it compiles as written, and below it what mLaTeX sets from it in your browser:

```latex title="quantikz.tex" preview
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{quantikz}
\begin{document}
\begin{quantikz}
\lstick{$\ket{0}$} & \gate{H} & \ctrl{1} & \meter{} \\
\lstick{$\ket{0}$} & \qw & \targ{} & \qw
\end{quantikz}
\end{document}
```
