## Paper and margins

The class's options set the paper (`a4paper`, `letterpaper`, `a5paper`, `legalpaper`) and the type size. `geometry` sets the rest:

```latex
\usepackage[a4paper, margin=2cm]{geometry}
\geometry{left=3cm, right=2cm}
```

`geometry` reads `paperwidth`, `paperheight`, `margin`, `hmargin`, `vmargin`, `left`, `right`, `top` and `bottom`, and a later, more specific key wins over an earlier, more general one, as in LaTeX. LaTeX's own lengths mean what they mean there too: `\setlength{\topmargin}{...}`, `\oddsidemargin`, `\textwidth`, `\headsep` and `\footskip`. `fullpage` and `a4wide` are accepted.

## Columns

`twocolumn` as a class option, `\twocolumn[...]` with a full-width part above, `\onecolumn`, and `multicol`'s `multicols` block. Columns are balanced on the last page. `figure*` and `table*` span both columns.

## Heads and feet

```latex
\usepackage{fancyhdr}
\usepackage{lastpage}
\pagestyle{fancy}
\fancyhf{}
\fancyhead[L]{\textit{Annual report}}
\fancyhead[R]{\leftmark}
\fancyfoot[C]{Page \thepage\ of \pageref{LastPage}}
```

`\pagestyle`, `\thispagestyle`, `fancyhdr`, KOMA-Script's `scrlayer-scrpage` and `titleps` set heads and feet. `\markboth` and `\markright` set what `\leftmark` and `\rightmark` show. `\pagenumbering{roman}` and `\setcounter{page}{n}` change the page numbers.

## Page breaks

`\newpage`, `\clearpage`, `\cleardoublepage` and `\pagebreak` break pages, and `\vfill` and `\vspace{\fill}` share out what a page leaves. A page holding nothing but a numbering change or a contents entry is never shipped as a blank page.

## The PDF

mLaTeX writes the PDF itself:

* **Fonts** are embedded, cut down to the glyphs the document uses.
* **Pictures**: JPEGs are embedded unchanged; PNG and WebP are compressed; PDF pages, EPS and SVG pictures are embedded as vectors.
* **Links and bookmarks** come with `hyperref`; see [References and Links](/docs/writing/references).
* **Information.** `\hypersetup`'s `pdftitle`, `pdfauthor`, `pdfsubject` and `pdfkeywords` are the file's title, author, subject and keywords.
* **The same document gives the same file.** The PDF's identifier is made from its own bytes.

```latex
\usepackage{hyperref}
\hypersetup{pdftitle={Energy Estimates}, pdfauthor={Mara Ellison}}
```

### PDF/A

For archiving, mLaTeX writes PDF/A-2b when asked, with the document's information as XMP metadata and an sRGB output intent:

```latex
\DocumentMetadata{pdfstandard=A-2b}
\documentclass{article}
```

`pdfx`'s `a-2b` option and its kin do the same.

### No file but the PDF

mLaTeX writes only the PDF: no `.aux`, `.log`, `.toc`, `.out` or `.bbl`. The PDF goes beside the document, or into the folder `--output-directory` names, under the document's name or the one `--job-name` gives. A program embedding mLaTeX can receive the PDF as bytes in memory instead; see [Embedding](/docs/embedding).
