Pictures written in TikZ and the packages built on it are drawn by mLaTeX directly, as vectors in the PDF. There is no PGF macro layer underneath, which is why a picture costs microseconds rather than seconds.

## TikZ

```latex
\usepackage{tikz}

\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=1.5]
  \draw[very thin, gray] (0,0) grid (3,2);
  \draw[thick, ->] (0,0) -- (3,0);
  \draw[blue!60, dashed] (0,0) rectangle (2,1);
  \draw[red] (1,1) circle (0.5);
  \node[draw, rounded corners] (a) at (0,2) {Start};
  \node[draw, right=of a] (b) {End};
  \draw[->] (a) -- node[above] {go} (b);
\end{tikzpicture}
```

### Pictures

`\begin{tikzpicture}[options] ... \end{tikzpicture}` sets a picture as a box in the text. `\tikz[options]{...}` sets a small one inline, and a `.tikz` file can be placed with `\includegraphics`.

### Drawing commands

| Command       | What it does                       |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| `\draw`       | Strokes a path.                    |
| `\fill`       | Fills a path.                      |
| `\filldraw`   | Fills a path and strokes its edge. |
| `\node`       | Places text, framed or not.        |
| `\coordinate` | Names a point without drawing it.  |

Colours by name and mixture, line widths, dashes and arrow tips, `-{Latex[length=2mm]}` included, are read from the options in brackets.

### Paths

* Lines with `--`, and corners with `|-` and `-|`.
* `rectangle`, `circle`, `ellipse`, `arc`, `grid` and `cycle`.
* Curves with `.. controls ..`, and `to[bend left]` or `to[out=,in=,looseness=]`.
* Relative points `+( )` and `++( )`, and polar points `(angle:radius)`.

### Nodes

* Shapes and looks: `draw`, `fill`, `circle`, `ellipse`, `rounded corners`, `dashed`, `double`.
* Sizes: `inner sep`, `minimum width`, `minimum height` and `text width`, with `font` and `align`.
* Anchors, such as `(a.north)` and `(a.30)`.
* Placing: `positioning`'s `right=of a`, with `node distance`.
* Labels on a line: `midway`, `pos=`, `near start`, `auto` and `swap`.

### Computing coordinates

* Coordinates may be formulas: `({1+1},{sin(30)*2})`.
* The `calc` library's `($(a)!0.5!(b)$)` and its kin.
* `let \p1 = (a) in ...`.
* `\foreach` with ranges, `count=` and several variables.
* `plot` of coordinates, or of a function over `domain` in `samples` steps.

### Structure

* `\matrix` of nodes and of math nodes.
* `edge` and `loop above`, for automata.
* Styles with `\tikzset` and `/.style`.
* `overlay` and `remember picture`, for pictures placed on the page.
* `baseline`, for a picture inline with text.

### Keys that are not drawn

A key mLaTeX does not draw is a warning, and the picture is drawn without it.

## pgfplots

```latex
\usepackage{pgfplots}

\begin{tikzpicture}
  \begin{axis}[xlabel=$x$, ylabel=$f(x)$, grid=major, legend pos=north west]
    \addplot[blue, domain=0:2, samples=50] {x^2};
    \addplot[red, only marks] coordinates {(0,0) (1,1) (2,4)};
    \legend{$x^2$, data}
  \end{axis}
\end{tikzpicture}
```

### Axes

`axis`, `semilogxaxis`, `semilogyaxis` and `loglogaxis`, with ticks, labels, titles, grids and `axis lines=left` or `middle`.

### \addplot

`\addplot[options]` draws one plot in an axis:

* of `coordinates {(0,0) (1,1)}`;
* of a table, inline or in a file;
* of a function, such as `{x^2}`, or a parametric one.

Marks, `only marks`, dashes and widths are read, and `ybar` with `symbolic x coords` draws bar charts.

### Legends

`\legend{first, second}` names the plots in order, placed by `legend pos`.

### Error bars

Error bars, with `y dir=both` and explicit, fixed or relative amounts.

### Filling between plots

`fill between` two named plots, with `soft clip`.

### Three dimensions

`\addplot3` of coordinates, tables or functions of x and y, as `surf` or `mesh`, seen from any `view`, with colormaps (`hot`, `jet`, `viridis`, `cool` and more) and a `colorbar`.

### What is ignored

Options that set styles for every plot at once, and group plots, are read and ignored.

## Commutative diagrams

### tikz-cd

```latex
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{tikzcd}
  A \arrow[r, "f"] \arrow[d, "g"'] & B \arrow[d, "h"] \\
  C \arrow[r, "k"'] & D
\end{tikzcd}
```

`\arrow[r]` points right, `d` down, `l` left and `u` up, and letters combine, as in `dr`. A label in quotes is set beside the arrow; a `'` after it puts it on the other side. `hook`, `two heads`, `mapsto`, `dashed`, `bend left`, `shift left`, `phantom` and the rest are drawn.

### xy-pic and amscd

xy-pic's `\xymatrix`, with its labels, shapes, curves and framed objects, and amscd's `CD` are drawn too.

### In a display

A diagram alone in a display is centred on its equation number.

***

## Circuits and trees

### Quantum circuits

`quantikz` and Qcircuit's `\Qcircuit`: wires, boxed gates, controls joined to targets, swaps, meters, classical wires and labels.

### Trees

`forest`'s `[S [NP] [VP]]`, and `qtree`'s and `tikz-qtree`'s `\Tree [.S the cat ]`, each parent centred over its children.

### Feynman diagrams

`tikz-feynman`'s `\feynmandiagram` and `axodraw2`'s pictures. `feynmp`'s diagrams, which need MetaPost, are not drawn; their place is kept.

***

## LaTeX's own picture

```latex
\setlength{\unitlength}{1cm}
\begin{picture}(4,2)
  \put(0,0){\line(1,1){2}}
  \put(2,1){\circle{1}}
  \put(3,0){\vector(0,1){2}}
\end{picture}
```

### The picture block

`\begin{picture}(width,height)` takes its size in `\unitlength`, and everything in it is placed with `\put(x,y){...}`.

### What it draws

`\put`, `\multiput`, `\line`, `\vector`, `\circle`, `\oval`, `\qbezier` and the framed boxes are drawn.

### PSTricks

PSTricks' `pspicture`, with its lines, circles, frames and labels, is drawn the same way.

## Other drawing packages

| Package        | How it is drawn                                             |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `pgf`          | Its basic layer's paths, as the TikZ paths they make        |
| `tkz-euclide`  | Points, segments, lines and circles of Euclidean geometry   |
| `smartdiagram` | The list's items joined by arrows                           |
| `pgfgantt`     | A table of tasks and their spans                            |
| `bohr`         | An atom's nucleus and electron shells                       |
| `circuitikz`   | Loads as TikZ; its component symbols are not drawn          |
| `modiagram`    | Its levels are read; the diagram's place is kept as a frame |
