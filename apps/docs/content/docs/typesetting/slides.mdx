The `beamer` class sets each frame as a page of its own, 128 by 96 millimetres, in eleven-point sans serif.

```latex title="talk.tex"
\documentclass{beamer}
\usetheme{Madrid}
\title[Energy Estimates]{Energy Estimates for Navier--Stokes}
\author{Mara Ellison}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\frame{\titlepage}

\begin{frame}{Motivation}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item<1-> Smooth solutions satisfy an energy equality
    \item<2-> The decay is exponential without forcing
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Main result}
  \begin{block}{Theorem}
    The kinetic energy decays as $e^{-\nu\lambda_1 t}$.
  \end{block}
\end{frame}
\end{document}
```

## Frames

### The frame block

`\begin{frame}{Title} ... \end{frame}` is one frame. Its title is its argument or the `\frametitle` it opens with, set at its head. What it holds is centred in the rest of the frame, or placed at the top with `\begin{frame}[t]`.

### The title page

`\frame{\titlepage}` sets the title page, with the title, subtitle, author, institute and date the preamble gives.

### Blocks

`block`, `alertblock` and `exampleblock` set their text under a coloured bar holding their title:

```latex
\begin{alertblock}{Warning}
  The scheme is only conditionally stable.
\end{alertblock}
```

### Columns

`columns` sets its `column` blocks side by side, each a width of its own. `\column{0.5\textwidth}` starts the next one without a block:

```latex
\begin{columns}
  \begin{column}{0.5\textwidth} Left \end{column}
  \begin{column}{0.5\textwidth} Right \end{column}
\end{columns}
```

### Outlines

`\tableofcontents` in a frame sets the talk's outline, and `\AtBeginSection`'s frames are set at the start of each section.

### Theorems and proofs

Theorems and proofs are set as in an article.

## Overlays

A frame becomes as many slides as its overlays ask for, each a page of the PDF.

### Overlay specifications

The steps in angle brackets are read as beamer reads them:

| Specification | Slides                                    |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `<2>`         | Slide 2                                   |
| `<2->`        | Slide 2 and every one after it            |
| `<-3>`        | Slides 1 to 3                             |
| `<1-3,5>`     | Slides 1 to 3, and 5                      |
| `<+->`        | From the next step on, one step at a time |
| `<.->`        | From the current step on                  |
| `<+(1)->`     | From one step after the next              |

### \pause

`\pause` hides the rest of the frame until the next step; `\pause[3]` names the step.

### Showing and hiding

| Command                                  | Effect                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `\only<2>{...}`                          | Present on slide 2 only, taking no room on the others               |
| `\uncover<2->{...}`, `\visible<2->{...}` | Keeps its room on every slide, seen from slide 2                    |
| `\invisible<2>{...}`                     | The other way round                                                 |
| `\onslide<2->{...}`                      | Like `\uncover`, or sets everything after it when given no argument |
| `\alt<2>{a}{b}`, `\temporal`             | One thing on some slides, another on the rest                       |

What a slide does not show keeps its place, so the slides of a frame line up. `onlyenv`, `uncoverenv`, `visibleenv` and `invisibleenv` work as blocks.

### Lists step by step

`\item<2->` shows an item from slide 2, and `\begin{itemize}[<+->]` shows one item per step.

### Highlighting

`\alert<2>{...}` highlights its text on slide 2. `\textbf<2>`, `\textit`, `\emph`, `\color` and `\textcolor` take overlays too, and so do blocks.

## Themes

### \usetheme

`\usetheme{Madrid}` draws a theme's head and foot, frame titles on a band or in the structure's colour, its bullets and its colours:

Madrid, AnnArbor, Boadilla, CambridgeUS, Warsaw, Copenhagen, Luebeck, Malmoe, Berlin, Darmstadt, Ilmenau, Frankfurt, Dresden, Singapore, Szeged, Antibes, JuanLesPins, Montpellier, Rochester, Pittsburgh, the sidebar themes and metropolis.

### Colour themes

`\usecolortheme{whale}` changes only the colours: whale, dolphin, seahorse, beaver, crane, wolverine, spruce, dove, seagull, fly and more.

### Outer and inner themes

`\useoutertheme` and `\useinnertheme` choose the head and foot, or the bullets and blocks, alone.

### What the foot carries

A short title, author or date, as in `\title[Short]{...}`, is what the foot carries.

### Themes mLaTeX does not know

A theme mLaTeX does not know is drawn as the default, with a warning.

<Callout type="info">
  A sidebar theme's sidebar is not drawn yet; its frames are set without it.
</Callout>
