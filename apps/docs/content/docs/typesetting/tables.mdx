## A table

```latex
\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Convergence of the projection scheme}
  \label{tab:convergence}
  \begin{tabular}{lrr}
    \hline
    Mesh  & Error           & Rate \\
    \hline
    $h$   & $2.1\times10^{-3}$ & --   \\
    $h/2$ & $5.3\times10^{-4}$ & 1.99 \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
```

### tabular

`\begin{tabular}{columns}` takes one letter for each column, then the rows: `&` between cells and `\\` at the end of a row.

| Column     | Contents                                        |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `l`        | Left-aligned                                    |
| `c`        | Centred                                         |
| `r`        | Right-aligned                                   |
| `p{width}` | A paragraph of that width, wrapped, top-aligned |
| `\|`       | A vertical rule between columns                 |

### Rules

`\hline` draws a rule across the table, and `\cline{2-3}` across columns 2 to 3 only.

### Spanning columns

`\multicolumn{2}{c}{Heading}` makes one cell across two columns, with its own alignment.

### The table float

The `table` block places the table and gives it its caption and label. See [Pictures and Floats](/docs/typesetting/pictures) for how floats are positioned.

## booktabs

`booktabs`' rules are drawn with the weights and spacing booktabs gives them, heavy at the top and bottom and light between.

```latex
\usepackage{booktabs}

\begin{tabular}{lr}
  \toprule
  Name & Value \\
  \midrule
  Mass & 2.3   \\
  \cmidrule(lr){1-2}
  Charge & 1.6 \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
```

### \toprule, \midrule and \bottomrule

The rule above the table, the rule under its head, and the rule closing it.

### \cmidrule

`\cmidrule{1-2}` draws a light rule across some columns. `(lr)` trims it at the left and right, so rules over columns side by side do not touch.

## array

`array`'s additions are read by `tabular` itself.

### m and b columns

`m{width}` is a paragraph column centred against the row, and `b{width}` one aligned at its bottom.

### Before and after a column

`>{...}` runs code before every cell of a column and `<{...}` after it, as in `>{\bfseries}l` for a bold column.

### Between columns

`@{...}` replaces the space between two columns with what it holds, and `!{...}` puts something between them while keeping the space.

### Column types of your own

`\newcolumntype` names a column type:

```latex
\usepackage{array}
\newcolumntype{C}[1]{>{\centering\arraybackslash}p{#1}}
\begin{tabular}{C{3cm}C{3cm}} ... \end{tabular}
```

## Tables to a width

### tabularx

`tabularx` takes a width and shares what the other columns leave among its `X` columns:

```latex
\usepackage{tabularx}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
  Name & A description long enough to wrap onto a second line. \\
\end{tabularx}
```

### tabulary

`tabulary`'s columns `L`, `C`, `R` and `J` share the width by how much text they hold.

### tabu

`\begin{tabu} to \linewidth {X[l] X[c] X[r]}` sets a table to a width with `X` columns aligned as given.

### tabularray

tabularray's `tblr`, with `colspec=`, is read as the tabular it describes.

## Tables over several pages

### longtable

`longtable` is set a row at a time, so a page can end between any two rows:

```latex
\usepackage{longtable}

\begin{longtable}{lr}
  \caption{Prices} \\
  \hline Item & Price \\ \hline
  \endfirsthead
  \hline Item & Price \\ \hline
  \endhead
  \hline
  \endfoot
  Tea  & 2 \\
  Cake & 3 \\
\end{longtable}
```

### Heads and feet

* The rows before `\endhead` repeat at the top of every page the table continues on.
* The rows before `\endfoot` close every page it breaks off.
* `\endfirsthead` and `\endlastfoot` give the first and last pages their own.

### Other long tables

`longtable*`, `supertabular`, `xtabular`, `xltabular`, tabularray's `longtblr` and tabu's `longtabu` break the same way.

## Cells

### multirow

`\multirow{2}{*}{Text}` makes one cell down two rows. It stands in the middle of the rows it spans, or level with the first for `[t]` and the last for `[b]`:

```latex
\usepackage{multirow}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
  \multirow{2}{*}{Fluids} & Water \\
                          & Oil   \\
\end{tabular}
```

### makecell

`\makecell{Two\\lines}` puts several lines in one cell, and `\thead` sets a bold head.

### diagbox

`\diagbox{Row}{Column}` splits a cell by a diagonal, a label either side.

### colortbl

`\rowcolor`, `\cellcolor` and `\columncolor` colour rows, cells and columns. Rules stay black.

```latex
\usepackage{colortbl}
\begin{tabular}{lr}
  \rowcolor{lightgray} Item & Price \\
  Tea & \cellcolor{yellow} 2 \\
\end{tabular}
```

### threeparttable

`threeparttable` keeps a table, its caption and its notes together.

### bigstrut

`\bigstrut` makes a cell taller, for a cell a rule runs close to.

### Approximated packages

<Callout type="warn">
  `dcolumn`'s `D` column sets numbers centred rather than aligned on the decimal point, and `hhline`'s rules are drawn as one rule across the table.
</Callout>

## Tables from data

`csvsimple` and `datatool` are built in, so a table can be filled from a file of comma-, semicolon- or tab-separated values.

### csvsimple

`\csvautobooktabular{file}` sets a whole file as a booktabs table, its first line the head:

```latex
\usepackage{csvsimple}
\csvautobooktabular{results.csv}
```

`\csvreader` with `head to column names` sets each row as you write it.

### datatool

`\DTLloaddb` loads a file, and `\DTLforeach`, `\DTLsort` and `\DTLsumcolumn` go through, sort and add up its rows.

### pgfplotstable

pgfplotstable's `\pgfplotstabletypeset` sets a table from its file.

***

## Right to left

A table in Arabic or Hebrew runs right to left: its first column at the right, its rules and padding mirrored, as polyglossia sets it. See [Languages](/docs/typesetting/languages).
