# Privacy Policy

<small>*Last updated: October 17th, 2026.*</small>

This Privacy Policy explains what information mLaTeX ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use its online services, why, and what you can do about it. The services are:

* **The editor**, where you write, set and share LaTeX documents in the browser;
* **The registry**, where packages for mLaTeX are published and installed; and
* **The documentation site** at mlatex.com.

The mLaTeX engine you install on your own machine collects nothing and sends nothing to us.

The short version: we collect what we need to run these services, we don't sell it, we don't show ads, and your documents are set on your own device.

## 1. What we collect

### 1.1 What you give us

* **Account details:** your name, email address and password. We store only a salted hash of the password, never the password itself.
* **Your documents:** the files of documents you save online, and the people you share them with. Documents you never save online stay in your browser and never reach us.
* **Packages you publish:** their files, names, versions and descriptions, and your name or username as their publisher. These are public by design.
* **Messages:** what you write when you contact us, such as through the contact form or by email.
* **Docs chat questions:** what you ask the docs chat, so it can answer.

### 1.2 What we collect as you use the services

* **Usage and device information:** your IP address, browser, device type, the pages you visit and when, and errors the services run into.
* **Registry downloads:** which package versions are downloaded, and when, counted so publishers can see how their packages are used.
* **Security logs:** sign-ins, password resets and changes to your account, kept to protect it.

### 1.3 What we don't collect

* **The contents of the documents you set.** The editor typesets with mLaTeX compiled to WebAssembly, on your device. Compiling a document doesn't send its text to us.
* **Payment information.** The services are free and we take no payments.

## 2. How we use it

We use the information to:

* run the services: save, sync and share your documents, publish and serve packages, and answer your searches and docs chat questions;
* keep accounts and the services secure, and stop spam, abuse and malicious packages;
* answer you when you contact us;
* send you messages about your account, such as password resets, security alerts and changes to our terms. We send no marketing email unless you ask for it;
* understand how the services are used, in aggregate, so we can fix problems and improve them; and
* meet our legal obligations.

We don't sell your information, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't use your documents to train machine-learning models.

## 3. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

* **contract:** to provide the services you signed up for;
* **legitimate interests:** to keep the services secure, prevent abuse and improve them, in ways that don't override your rights;
* **consent:** where we ask for it, which you can withdraw at any time; and
* **legal obligation:** where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

## 4. Who we share it with

### 4.1 Service providers

We use a few companies to run the services. They process information only on our instructions:

| Provider   | What it does for us                                                        |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Vercel     | Hosts the websites and the editor                                          |
| Resend     | Sends email, such as contact form replies and account messages             |
| Typesense  | Powers search on the documentation site                                    |
| OpenRouter | Passes docs chat questions to the language model that answers them         |
| Upstash    | Rate-limits the docs chat to prevent abuse                                 |
| GitHub     | Hosts our source code, and supplies release and community data to the site |

We'll update this list when it changes.

### 4.2 Other people, as you choose

The people you share a document with can see it and your name. Packages you publish, with your publisher name, are visible to everyone.

### 4.3 When the law requires it

We disclose information when we believe in good faith that the law requires it, or that it's needed to protect someone's safety, to investigate abuse, or to defend our rights.

### 4.4 If the services change hands

If mLaTeX's services are transferred to another organization, your information may go with them, still covered by this policy, and we'll tell you before that happens.

## 5. How long we keep it

* **Account details:** for as long as you have the account. When you delete it, we delete them within 30 days, apart from what the law requires us to keep.
* **Documents:** until you delete them or your account. Copies in backups are gone within 30 days after that.
* **Published packages:** for as long as they're published, since other documents depend on them. Your publisher name stays on versions already published.
* **Logs and usage information:** up to 90 days, then deleted or kept only in aggregate form that doesn't identify you.
* **Docs chat questions and messages to us:** as long as we need them to answer and improve, and no more than a year.

## 6. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, hashed passwords, access limited to the people who need it, and monitoring for abuse. No service is perfectly secure, so if you find a weakness, please report it as our [security policy](https://github.com/modern-latex/mlatex/blob/main/SECURITY.md) explains.

## 7. International transfers

Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses for those transfers.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

* see the information we hold about you, and get a copy;
* correct it if it's wrong;
* delete it;
* export your documents in a portable form;
* object to or restrict how we use it; and
* withdraw consent you gave.

You can do most of this yourself in the editor: edit your account, export your documents, or delete them and your account. For anything else, write to [support@mlatex.com](mailto:support@mlatex.com). We'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

### California

California residents have the right to know what we collect and why, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information as California law defines those words.

## 9. Cookies and local storage

We use only what the services need to work:

* **A session cookie** to keep you logged in;
* **Your browser's storage** to keep your documents and settings, such as your theme, on your device; and
* **Security measures** that protect forms from abuse.

We use no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Blocking cookies will log you out, but you can still read the documentation.

## 10. Children

The services aren't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, tell us and we'll delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy in a material way, we'll tell account holders by email or in the services before the change takes effect, and update the date at the top.

## 12. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Write to [support@mlatex.com](mailto:support@mlatex.com).
